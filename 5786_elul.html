3.Psalm 27

1. לְדָוִד  יְהֹוָה  אוֹרִי וְיִשְׁעִי מִמִּי אִירָא יְהֹוָה מָעוֹז־חַיַּי מִמִּי אֶפְחָד׃

Of David.
GOD is my light and my help;
whom should I fear?
GOD is the stronghold of my life,
whom should I dread?

2. בִּקְרֹב עָלַי  מְרֵעִים לֶאֱכֹל אֶת־בְּשָׂרִי צָרַי וְאֹיְבַי לִי הֵמָּה כָשְׁלוּ וְנָפָלוּ׃

When evildoers draw near to devour my flesh
it is they, my foes and my enemies,
who stumble and fall.

3. אִם־תַּחֲנֶה עָלַי  מַחֲנֶה לֹא־יִירָא לִבִּי אִם־תָּקוּם עָלַי מִלְחָמָה בְּזֹאת אֲנִי בוֹטֵחַ׃

Should an army besiege me, my heart would have no fear;
should war engulf me, still would I be confident.

4. אַחַת  שָׁאַלְתִּי מֵאֵת־יְהֹוָה אוֹתָהּ אֲבַקֵּשׁ שִׁבְתִּי בְּבֵית־יְהֹוָה כׇּל־יְמֵי חַיַּי לַחֲזוֹת בְּנֹעַם־יְהֹוָה וּלְבַקֵּר בְּהֵיכָלוֹ׃

One thing I ask of God,
only that do I seek:
to live in God’s house
all the days of my life,
to gaze upon God’s beauty,
to frequent the temple.

5. כִּי יִצְפְּנֵנִי  בְּסֻכֹּה בְּיוֹם רָעָה יַסְתִּרֵנִי בְּסֵתֶר אׇהֳלוֹ בְּצוּר יְרוֹמְמֵנִי׃

I will be sheltered in God’s pavilion.
On an evil day, I will be granted the protection of God’s tent and raised up high upon a rock.

6. וְעַתָּה יָרוּם רֹאשִׁי עַל אֹיְבַי סְבִיבוֹתַי וְאֶזְבְּחָה בְאׇהֳלוֹ זִבְחֵי תְרוּעָה אָשִׁירָה וַאֲזַמְּרָה לַיהֹוָה׃

And now, let my head be lifted above my foes on all sides, and [then] I shall slaughter in that tent offerings of exultation. I shall sing and intone psalms to God.




7. שְׁמַע־יְהֹוָה קוֹלִי אֶקְרָא וְחׇנֵּנִי וַעֲנֵנִי׃

Hear, O God, my voice as I call; and be gracious unto me, and answer me.



8. לְךָ  אָמַר לִבִּי בַּקְּשׁוּ פָנָי אֶת־פָּנֶיךָ יְהֹוָה אֲבַקֵּשׁ׃

On Your behalf, my heart says: "Seek My presence [Israel]." [And, accordingly,] I will seek your presence, O God.



9. אַל־תַּסְתֵּר פָּנֶיךָ  מִמֶּנִּי אַל תַּט־בְּאַף עַבְדֶּךָ עֶזְרָתִי הָיִיתָ אַל־תִּטְּשֵׁנִי וְאַל־תַּעַזְבֵנִי אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׁעִי׃

Do not hide Your presence from me;
do not thrust aside Your servant in anger;
You have ever been my help.
Do not forsake me, do not abandon me,
O God, my deliverer.


10. כִּי־אָבִי וְאִמִּי עֲזָבוּנִי וַיהֹוָה יַאַסְפֵנִי׃

Though my father and my mother have left me, God will take me in.



11. הוֹרֵנִי יְהֹוָה דַּרְכֶּךָ וּנְחֵנִי בְּאֹרַח מִישׁוֹר לְמַעַן שׁוֹרְרָי׃

Teach me, O God, Your ways, and lead me in a straight path for the sake of [the undoing of] those who eye me [for evil].



12. אַל־תִּתְּנֵנִי בְּנֶפֶשׁ צָרָי כִּי קָמוּ־בִי עֵדֵי־שֶׁקֶר וִיפֵחַ חָמָס׃

Deliver me not to the desire of my foes; for false witnesses have risen against me, and spouters of violence.

13. לוּלֵא הֶאֱמַנְתִּי לִרְאוֹת בְּטוּב־יְהֹוָה בְּאֶרֶץ חַיִּים׃

[And they would already have destroyed me] had I not believed in seeing God’s goodness in the land of the living.



14. קַוֵּה אֶל־יְהֹוָה חֲזַק וְיַאֲמֵץ לִבֶּךָ וְקַוֵּה אֶל־יְהֹוָה׃

Look to God; be strong, and let your heart take courage, and [if your prayer is not answered, then, again,] hope to God.

12.Adon Haselichot (M-anon/Mishkan)

Awesome God, Beneficent and good to all your creatures, Clothed in right, Declaring truth, God we have sinned before you; have mercy on us.

הָדוּר בְּנִפְלָאוֹת. וָתִיק בְּנֶֽחָמוֹת. זוֹכֵר בְּרִית אָבוֹת. חוֹקֵר כְּלָיוֹת׃ חָטָֽאנוּ לְפָנֶֽיךָ רַחֵם עָלֵֽינוּ׃

hadur b'nifla'ot vatik b'nechamot zocheir b'rit avot chokeir k'layot (chatanu l'faneicha racheim aleinu)(2x)

Ever constant in comforts, Foreseeing, Glorious in wonders, Hearkening to prayers, God we have sinned before you; have mercy on us.

טוֹב וּמֵטִיב לַבְּרִיּוֹת. יוֹדֵעַ כָּל־נִסְתָּרוֹת. כּוֹבֵשׁ עֲוֺנוֹת. לוֹבֵשׁ צְדָקוֹת׃ חָטָֽאנוּ לְפָנֶֽיךָ רַחֵם עָלֵֽינוּ׃

tov umeitiv labriyot yodei'a kol nistarot koveish avonot loveish ts'dakot (chatanu l'faneicha racheim aleinu)(2x)

Invoking the generations, Judging in knowledge, Knowing all that is hidden, Lord of forgiveness. Moving over the skies, God we have sinned before you; have mercy on us.

מָלֵא זָֽכִיּוֹת.נוֹרָא תְהִלּוֹת. סוֹלֵֽחַ עֲוֺנוֹת. עוֹנֶה בַצָּרוֹת׃ חָטָֽאנוּ לְפָנֶֽיךָ רַחֵם עָלֵֽינוּ׃

malei zachiyot nora t'hilot solei'ach avonot oneh batsarot (chatanu l'faneicha racheim aleinu)(2x)

Nigh in trouble, overflowing in virtue, Pardoning sins, Quelling sinfulness, Remembering the covenant with the fathers, God we have sinned before you;have mercy on us.

פּוֹעֵל יְשׁוּעוֹת.צוֹפֶה עֲתִידוֹת.קוֹרֵא הַדּוֹרוֹת.רוֹכֵב עֲרָבוֹת.שׁוֹמֵֽעַ תְּפִלּוֹת.תְּמִים דֵּעוֹת׃חָטָֽאנוּ לְפָנֶֽיךָרַחֵם עָלֵֽינוּ׃

po'eil yeshu'ot tsofeh atidot koreih hadorot rocheiv aravot shomei'a t'filot t'mim dei'ot (chatanu l'faneicha racheim aleinu)(2x)

Searcher of inmost feelings, Tester of hearts, Uncovering the depths, Venerated in praises, Working salvation, God we have sinned before you;have mercy on us.

((ya'aleh)(3x) ya'aleh koleinu)(2x)
[niggun]

Let our needs rise up with the darkness,
our cries with the rays of the sun.
All day our praise is before you,
songs of joy and bliss till evening comes.
From the pain, it will resurrect me
And fill me with inspiration.
